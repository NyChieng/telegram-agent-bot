# Safety Policy

The bot applies a simple pre-LLM safety filter. It blocks or redirects requests involving:

- explicit sexual content
- sexual content involving minors
- hate speech
- harassment
- calls for violence
- political extremism
- impersonating Mao Zedong as the real person
- generating real-world political propaganda

When blocked, the bot should give a short, funny refusal and redirect to safe help.
